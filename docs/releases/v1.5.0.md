# Daytile 1.5.0

Changing day now follows your finger. Nothing else changes.

## New

- Drag sideways and the day's blocks move with your finger, with the next day's blocks coming in beside them. Let go a short way along, or flick, and the day changes. Let go sooner, or flick back, and it returns.
- The heading leans after your finger and changes to the next day, with the usual tick, at the point where letting go would change day.
- A day chosen from the calendar, or reached with Back, slides in the same way.
- With animations switched off in the system settings, the blocks still follow your finger and nothing moves by itself.

The APK is 116 KB, up from 113 KB.

Daytile remains offline, with no account, network access, analytics or logging. Tested on Android 17.

Install `daytile-1.5.0.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.

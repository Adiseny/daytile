# Daytile 1.6.1

- Faster group moves on crowded days, with one shared overlap check. Small days need no scratch array; older block times keep the same rules.
- Faster selection lookups in drawing, checks and bulk saves, with cached drag bounds. Lifted blocks remain available while scrolling through crowded days, and edits update their bounds immediately. The redundant linear lookup helper is removed.
- Crowded weeks keep nearby, long and held tiles. Both pages stay current during week swipes; renaming keeps existing overlap layouts.
- Moving week tiles and carrying compact day tiles reuse their drawing. Contrast colours are cached and duplicate background blending is removed.
- Cross-day moves copy and sort affected lists once and update both days in one screen refresh.

Features, appearance, reminders and task storage are unchanged. The signed APK is 141,149 bytes, 1,120 bytes (0.8%) larger than 1.6.0. No dependencies or stored data are added.

All 77 unit and 75 Android tests pass on Android 17. Sixteen day and week captures match 1.6.0 pixel for pixel in both palettes. Privacy, lint and release-signature checks pass.

Install `daytile-1.6.1.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.

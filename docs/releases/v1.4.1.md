# Daytile 1.4.1

Daytile stores less. The planner's interface and features are unchanged.

## Smaller

- The app's data is about 37 KB after a first use, down from 78 KB. Updating shrinks an existing install to match.
- Saved plans take a third less space. A year of eight blocks a day is about 100 KB, down from 150 KB.
- The APK is 114 KB, down from 120 KB.

## Fix

- Entering a title from a physical keyboard no longer leaves the day shaded until the next touch.

Plans opened by 1.4.1 cannot be opened by earlier versions.

Daytile remains offline, with no account, network access, analytics or logging. Tested on Android 17.

Install `daytile-1.4.1.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.

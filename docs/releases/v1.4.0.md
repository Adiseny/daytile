# Daytile 1.4.0

A much smaller Daytile. The planner's interface and features are unchanged.

## Smaller

- The interface is rebuilt on Android's own views, and the app no longer includes any libraries. The APK is 120 KB, down from 958 KB.
- Installed, the app takes about 140 KB, and at most 400 KB once Android has compiled it. Version 1.3.3 took 2.8 MB, then 7.1 MB.
- Saved plans take about 40% less space. A year of eight blocks a day is about 160 KB.

## Faster and lighter

- Launch and scrolling do less work on the main thread: about a fifth less at launch and about half while scrolling.
- Saving a change and refreshing its day take one trip to a background thread, and reminders are updated once the screen has its answer.
- With reminders off, a restart or a clock change no longer starts the app.
- A reminder that has not changed is no longer posted again.

Plans opened by 1.4.0 cannot be opened by earlier versions.

Daytile remains offline, with no account, network access, analytics or logging. Tested on Android 17.

Install `daytile-1.4.0.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.

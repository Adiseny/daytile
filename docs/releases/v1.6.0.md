# Daytile 1.6.0

A week view, and quicker ways to add and move blocks.

## New

- Pinch in for the week: seven days side by side, coloured by the time of day. Swipe to change week, tap a date to open it, and pinch out to go back to the day you were on.
- In the week, tap empty time to add a block, hold a block to move it to another day or time, and hold its lower edge to make it longer or shorter.
- Move several blocks at once: hold two together to lift them, tap others to lift them too, then drag any of them.
- Add blocks in a row: the keyboard's key adds a block and stays for the next, which starts where the last ended.

## Changed

- Reminders come ten minutes before a block, not five, and read “Next up at 15:20”, then “Until 16:05”, above the block's name.
- Resizing a block no longer looks or feels like lifting it, and no longer sets the day scrolling by itself.

The APK is 140 KB, up from 116 KB.

Daytile remains offline, with no account, network access, analytics or logging. Tested on Android 17.

Install `daytile-1.6.0.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.

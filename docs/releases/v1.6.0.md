# Daytile 1.6.0

A week view, and quicker ways to add and move blocks.

## New

- Week view. Pinch in to see the week: seven days side by side, coloured by the time of day. Swipe to change week, tap a date to open that day, and pinch out or press Back to return to the day you were on.
- In the week, tap empty time to add a block, tap a block for its actions, hold a block to move it to another day or time, and hold its lower edge to make it longer or shorter.
- Move several blocks at once. Hold two together to lift them, tap others to lift them too, then drag any of them. Tap empty time or press Back to put them down.
- Add blocks in a row. The keyboard's key adds a block and stays for the next, which starts where the last ended. The Add button still adds and closes.

## Changed

- Reminders come ten minutes before a block, not five. They read “Next up at 15:20” before a block and “Until 16:05” while it runs, above the block's name.
- Resizing a block no longer looks or feels like lifting it: the block stays solid, there is no long buzz, and only its handle darkens.

## Fixed

- Holding a block's handle near the bottom of the screen no longer sets the day scrolling by itself, and the day scrolls more slowly while you resize.
- A touch straight after scrolling no longer resizes a block by accident.
- A second finger on the screen no longer changes the day.

In the week, a screen reader can move a block by a day or a quarter of an hour, lengthen or shorten it, and open any day.

The APK is 140 KB, up from 116 KB.

Daytile remains offline, with no account, network access, analytics or logging. Tested on Android 17.

Install `daytile-1.6.0.apk` over the existing app to retain your plans. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.
